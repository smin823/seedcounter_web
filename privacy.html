Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the SeedCounter app collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. SeedCounter does not require an account, login, or email address.

1. Who We Are

SeedCounter is provided by Plolabs ("we," "our," or "us"). We are responsible for the processing of personal information described in this Privacy Policy. You can reach us at contact@plolabs.com.

2. Photos and Count Results Stay on Your Device

Photos taken with the camera, photos you choose to import from your photo library, and their count results (for seed scanning and counting) are handled as follows:

  • Images are analyzed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Results you save are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you delete them in the album or uninstall the app.
  • The app only reads the photos you pick from your photo library. It does not scan or upload your library.
  • When you choose to share a result, the image is passed to the app or service you select, and that recipient's own policy applies.

3. Information Collected Automatically

The following information is collected through the third-party services listed in Section 5:

  • Identifiers: an anonymous app user ID used to manage your subscription, an app installation ID, and the device advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android).
  • Purchase history: the subscription product and its start, renewal, and expiration dates. Payment details are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store and are never shared with us.
  • App activity: how features are used (for example, screens viewed, counts completed, and ad interactions).
  • Diagnostics: crash logs, error logs, and device information such as model, operating system version, and available memory.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, used by advertising and analytics services. We do not collect precise (GPS) location.

4. How We Use Information

  • To provide the app, including subscriptions, the daily free usage limit, and rewards from rewarded ads.
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it (analytics).
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To show and measure ads for free users, which may be personalized where permitted.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and to keep the service secure.

5. Third-Party Services

We share information with the following companies only as needed for the purposes above. Each processes information under its own privacy policy.

  • Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics): analytics and crash reporting.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management.
  • Google AdMob: advertising.
  • Ad networks that serve ads through AdMob mediation: Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Liftoff (Vungle), InMobi, and ironSource (Unity).

These companies operate servers in the United States and other countries, so your information may be transferred outside your country. Information is sent over encrypted connections while you use the app. You can limit what is shared for advertising by using the choices in Section 6, or by subscribing to Premium, which shows no ads.

6. Your Advertising Choices

  • In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app menu under Privacy settings.
  • On iOS, the app asks for permission before tracking. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads).
  • Premium subscribers do not see ads.

7. Retention

Information on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. Information held by the third-party services in Section 5 is kept for the periods set by each service and then deleted or anonymized. We do not keep a separate copy on our own servers.

8. Security

Information sent from the app is encrypted in transit. Because photos are processed on your device, they are not exposed to network risks.

9. Children

SeedCounter is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your Rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information by contacting us at contact@plolabs.com. Because the app has no accounts, we cannot identify you from your name or email; we will help you make your request to the services that hold the information. You can also delete data on your device at any time by deleting album items or uninstalling the app, and reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will post the updated version in the app and on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app or on its store listing.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us at:
